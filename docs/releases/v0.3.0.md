# Smooth64 — Cinder Caldera

Download **Smooth64-Browser.zip**, extract it, and double-click **Smooth64-Play.html**. Or download the HTML file directly and open it in Edge, Chrome, or Firefox. No installation or server needed. Choose **Cinder Caldera** on the welcome screen.

## A full level

Climb a volcanic crater to the **Ember Star** on top of the central Spire. The route circles the crater through six sections:

- **Ashfall Landing:** the start, and a broken gate you can wall-kick up.
- **Basalt Steps:** columns shrink as the gaps over the lava widen, finishing with a long jump.
- **The Forge:** a ledge-grab rampart and a step that is too high to jump, so you need a backflip.
- **The Chimney:** 1,160 units of consecutive wall kicks.
- **Chain Bridge:** hang from grates between chain posts.
- **Obsidian Ridge:** narrow beams and blades, then a very slippery chute.
- **The Spire:** a spiral of narrowing ledges, a wall-kick corridor, a backflip step, and a jump for the summit rim.

- **Lava** is the core's own `SURFACE_BURNING`. A touch costs three wedges of health and launches you upward. **Coins heal one wedge**, like the original.
- **Eight Ember Shards**, each behind an optional challenge. Finding all eight lights the **Crimson Star** on a lava altar that takes a long jump to reach.
- **Five checkpoint beacons.** Touch one to make it your respawn point; **R** returns you there. Falling at zero health does too.
- A power meter, coin/shard/star counts, a run timer with best time, a skippable intro camera, and a star-get summary.

Every leg of the main route and every shard is proven reachable in the test suite by driving the real movement core with controller inputs.

## Movement presentation

- Changes of action now crossfade over 0.12 s of display time instead of snapping in a single tick.
- The side somersault turns back around through its landing instead of snapping 180°.
- The slide kick is now a low, feet-first slide rather than a standing kick.
- The ground pound lands on the seat that the core's recovery starts from.
- Letting go of the stick at walking speed keeps the walk cycle.
- Fall-damage knockbacks topple over instead of running.
- Rollouts somersault.
- Single and double jumps use distinct poses that blend continuously through the apex. There are also new lava-boost and hard-knockback poses.
- Dust on takeoff and landing, sparks on wall kicks, a ground-pound shockwave, skid dust, and fire on lava boosts.
- The camera rises to look down narrow shafts instead of pressing against the explorer's back.

The movement actions are unchanged. The host adds one function, `s64_heal`, which queues the heal counter the way the original coin interaction does. Native and WASM builds agree byte-for-byte on 1,500 more ticks in the caldera, including heals.

Controls: **WASD** move · **Space** jump · **Shift** crouch · **J** punch/dive · **drag** orbit · **R** checkpoint · **P** pause.
