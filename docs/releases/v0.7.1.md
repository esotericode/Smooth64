# Smooth64 — Controller menus

Every menu now works from a gamepad, coins sound gentler, and the far peaks in Hoarfrost Heights stop flickering.

## Play

Download **Smooth64-Browser.zip**, extract it, and open **Smooth64-Play.html**.
Or download the HTML file directly. Both work offline, with no setup or ROM. Sound and music start after your first click, tap, or key press (a browser rule).

## What's new

- **Menus from a gamepad.** On the welcome screen, in the pause menu, the move guide and the star screen:
  - the D-pad or left stick moves between choices, and repeats like an arrow key while held;
  - **A** selects, **B** goes back (it closes the move guide first, then the menu), and **Start** opens or closes the menu;
  - left or right sets a slider, and **A** flips a switch;
  - long pages scroll a step at a time as you move, so the move guide can be read on the way down;
  - an orange ring shows where you are; it steps aside as soon as you use the mouse, touch or keyboard.
- **A gentler coin.** The bright arcade ding is replaced by two soft mallet notes a fifth apart, generated in code. It rings around 520 Hz instead of 3 kHz and plays 5 dB quieter, so a hundred coins in a row stay pleasant. The star jingle's closing sparkle uses the same chime.
- **Steady far peaks.** In Hoarfrost Heights the distant mountains' snow caps sat right on their rock, which the depth buffer cannot separate that far away, so their tops shimmered. Each is now a rock base and a snow cap that meet at one ring. The frozen falls' glint, the hut's windows and painted titles are held steady too.

## Verification

All 15 native and 83 Node tests pass. New tests cover the gamepad's menu input (presses, repeats, held buttons, Start), how focus picks the next control, and the coin chime's length, pitch and level. The browser check now plays through the menus with a mocked gamepad alone: choosing a world, changing settings and sliders, scrolling the move guide, travelling to a checkpoint and resuming. Narrow-lens renders of the distant peaks confirmed the flicker is gone. This is automated verification, not a session with a physical controller.

Controls: **WASD** move · **Space** jump · **Shift** crouch · **J** punch/dive · **drag** orbit · **R** checkpoint · **P** pause · gamepad: left stick, **A**, **X**/**B**, triggers, **Start**.
