# Smooth64 — Hands, feet & new heights

Download **Smooth64-Browser.zip**, extract it, and double-click **Smooth64-Play.html**. Or download the HTML file directly and open it in Edge, Chrome, or Firefox. No installation or server needed.

- Orb gloves and little shoes, with custom poses for running, punches, kicks, flips, long jumps, dives, slides, crouching, landings, ceiling hangs, and ledge climbs.
- Ledge hangs now put the body below the lip and keep the hands anchored. The camera follows the visible character. A live arrow shows the camera-relative direction to climb.
- Four connected new areas: **Ledge garden**, **Wall-kick tower**, **Skyline circuit**, and **Canopy walk**. The original six areas are intact.
- **15 collectible sparks**, route counts, and a reset-sparks button. Respawning keeps collected sparks for the current session.

**Ledge controls:** release the stick to hang; push toward the platform to climb after a brief pause; press **A / Space** for a quick climb. Pull away or press **Z / Shift** to drop. The reported all-directions-drop behavior did not reproduce in the regression scenarios; the original controls are retained.

The two development branches are integrated: animation metadata and secondary poses, geometry batching, local shadows, and the canopy route join the anchored ledge rig and compact courses. The large expanse layout remains in branch history.

The C movement core and compiled WASM are unchanged. The update passed 13 native behavioral tests and 15 Node tests, including 5,592 ticks of native/WASM state comparison, ledge cases at four camera orientations, a complete wall-tower ascent, and hand-anchor checks. Physical gamepad and visual browser QA remain unverified for this edition in the development environment.

Controls: **WASD** move · **Space** jump · **Shift** crouch · **J** punch/dive · **drag** orbit · **R** respawn · **P** pause. Choose areas **07–10** in the left destination panel for the new courses.
