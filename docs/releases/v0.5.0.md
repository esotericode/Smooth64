# Smooth64 — Quiet trails

Four original, mellow tracks bring a little late-1990s console atmosphere to both worlds:

- **Mosslight Path** — soft wooden mallets and warm chords.
- **Clouds Beyond the Ridge** — airy flute and open space.
- **Embers at Rest** — rounded electric keys and a gentle minor-key melody.
- **Paper Lantern Sky** — a quiet music box with flute answers.

Each piece runs about two minutes. Tracks are shuffled without immediate repeats and blend into one another over twelve seconds. Sparse melodies and light percussion keep the soundtrack in the background.

Music starts quietly at 30%. **Menu → Settings** has a music switch and volume slider, separate from sound effects. Music continues across world changes and retries, softens in menus, and fades out when the game window loses focus. Muting and returning keep your place. Preferences are remembered.

Everything works in the offline download, with no additional music downloads or accounts. This release also includes the sound effects, footstep timing, and adjustable gamepad dead zone introduced in v0.4.0.

Download and extract **Smooth64-Browser.zip**, then double-click **Smooth64-Play.html**. Start either world to hear the soundtrack.
