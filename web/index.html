     THE MOVE GUIDE

    Make your next move.

    Hold a direction to run. Add Alt for a gentle walk, or ease the gamepad stick forward.

    Put it together

    Double / triple jump
    Jump again just after landing. Keep running for the third jump.
    Long jump
    Run, crouch, then jump.
    Backflip
    Stand still, hold crouch, then jump.
    Side somersault
    Reverse direction while running. Jump during the turn.
    Wall kick
    Jump into a wall, then tap jump again on impact.
    Ledge climb
    Catch a lip while falling. Push toward the ledge to climb, or tap jump to pull up quickly. Pull away or crouch to drop.
    Ceiling hang
    Jump under a hanging frame. Keep holding jump as you move.
    Dive / ground pound
    Attack while moving fast in the air to dive. Crouch in the air to ground pound.

    Power, coins & checkpoints

    Coins restore one wedge of power. Lava burns three wedges and launches you upward. Touch a beacon to set your checkpoint. If you fall or run out of power, you return there with your collected items intact. These rules work in every world.

    Cinder Caldera

    Claim the Ember Star on the Spire's summit. Find eight red Ember Shards to reveal the Crimson Star on the lava altar below the landing.

    Hoarfrost Heights

    Claim the Icefall Star on the Shoulder of the Horn, then climb on to the Aurora Star on the summit and ride the Avalanche Run home. Eight blue Frost Shards reveal the Polar Star on the Mirror Isle. Blue ice will not hold you: you slide on it and cannot climb it, and a full run takes a long skid to stop. Let go of the stick as you land to stop dead. Deep snow slows your run; grey rock grips even when steep. On Gale Ridge the wind always blows the same way: standing still gets you blown off, so keep moving and jump each gap from its very edge. Open water bites like lava. Fall into the clouds and you return to your beacon.

    The Expanse

    A huge open world for testing size: rolling ground 240,000 units across, hills, a mountain, crates to climb and a coin trail out to each rim. Every travel point is open in the Menu. Jump off a rim and you return to your beacon.

    Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick orbits, A jumps, X/B attacks, either trigger crouches, Start opens the menu. In menus, the D-pad or left stick moves between choices, A selects, B goes back, and left or right sets a slider. If a stick drifts, raise the stick dead zone in Settings. Touch: left pad + A/B/Z. Sound and music volume are in Settings too.

    About the movement core

    Original US movement actions and math from n64decomp via libsm64, running in C at 30 ticks per second. Collision and presentation differ from the N64 game; complete equivalence is not verified. No ROM or Nintendo visual/audio assets are used. See the repository's fidelity notes.
