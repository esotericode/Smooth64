Put it together

Double / triple jump
Jump again just after landing; keep running for the third.
Long jump
Run → crouch → jump.
Backflip
Stand still → hold crouch → jump.
Side somersault
Run → reverse direction → jump during the turn.
Wall kick
Jump into a wall → press jump again on impact.
Ledge grab / climb
Catch a lip while descending. Release the stick to hang. Push toward the ledge to climb, or tap A / Space to pull up quickly. Pull away or press Z / Shift to drop. Stick climbing starts after a brief pause; the on-screen arrow follows your camera.
Ceiling hang
Hold A / Space under the green hanging frame. Keep holding it as you move.
Dive / ground pound
B while moving fast in the air / Z while airborne. At low speed, B gives a jump kick.

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick orbits, A jumps, X/B attacks, either trigger crouches. Touch: left pad + A/B/Z.

About the movement core

Original US movement actions and math from n64decomp via libsm64, running in C at 30 ticks per second. The collision adapter and host differ from the N64 game; complete 1:1 equivalence is not yet verified. No ROM or original visual/audio assets are used. See the repository’s fidelity notes for known limits.
